Google Space Invaders Arcade Game

How It Was Made

Concept Overview

Created by Tomohiro Nishikado in 1978, Space Invaders became a worldwide phenomenon with a simple, gripping loop — stop the descending aliens before they reach the bottom. Its chunky pixels and iconic beeps turned into pop-culture touchstones and helped bring video games to the mainstream.

Inspiration and Development

We built Google Space Invaders as a lighthearted homage with a clever twist: the letters of the Google logo double as your shields. It’s a nostalgia boost with a wink. This tribute builds on the excellent open-source work by N. Landsteiner (masswerk.at), who also contributed to our popular Google Terminal project.

The Easter Egg Experience

Key Features

Retro visuals, snappy controls, and that edge-of-your-seat pace you remember — plus a fresh idea: the G, o, o, g, l, e letters are destructible shields. You’re not just defending Earth; you’re protecting the Google name on the front line.

How It Works

Command your laser cannon through tightening waves of pixel invaders. Duck behind letter-shields for cover, time your shots, and push the swarm back before it reaches the bottom.

How to Play

  1. Click “Play” to start defending Google.
  2. Desktop: move with ←→ or AD; press Space to fire.
  3. Mobile: drag to move, tap to shoot.
  4. Each wave speeds up and hits harder — stay sharp.
  5. Use the Google letter shields wisely — they won’t last forever.

Like official Google Easter eggs — such as Zerg Rush (swarms of “o”s attacking your results), Atari Breakout (Google Images turned into bricks), and the Google Zipper — this project celebrates classic game ideas with a playful Google spin.

Final Thoughts

Google Space Invaders shows how timeless mechanics still spark new fun. It’s unofficial, but it captures the spirit of Google’s Easter eggs: familiar, cheeky, and instantly pick-up-and-play.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Google Space Invaders?

It’s a fan-made tribute that plays like classic Space Invaders — with a Google twist.

You pilot a cannon against descending pixel aliens while the Google logo letters act as your shields. Survive escalating waves and keep the swarm from breaking through.

It’s a sweet spot of arcade nostalgia and modern web magic — quick to learn, hard to put down.

How do I play the Google Space Invaders Easter egg?

Click “Play” to begin. On desktop, move with the arrow keys or A/D and fire with the spacebar. On mobile, drag to move and tap to shoot. Clear every alien before they reach the bottom, watch your lives, and chase a new high score — then dare your friends to beat it.
